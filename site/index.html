THEORICA is not built around a single flattering result.

The first major hypothesis failed.

A frozen holdout showed that our sophisticated active-sampling heuristic did not reliably beat a competent uniform experimental design. We kept the failure public. It exposed a deeper problem: an agent cannot discover a theory that its hypothesis language cannot express.
