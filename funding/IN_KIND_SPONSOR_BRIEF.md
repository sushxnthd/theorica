# THEORICA — In-Kind Physical-Lab Sponsorship Brief

## Ask
Provide **components, lab access, fabrication help, or a small unrestricted equipment grant** for a preregistered autonomous-science experiment.

No paid consulting or commercial service is being requested.

## Existing evidence
THEORICA is already evaluated on two independent native research benchmarks:

- **ACDB canonical paper panel:** directed F1 0.704 vs 0.709 for official PC-greedy, with 2.06 vs 2.54 interventions and higher ACDB efficiency.
- **DiscoverPhysics native trajectory evaluator:** a generic 15-experiment system-identification policy passed 4/5 compatible unfamiliar-physics worlds; the Coulomb failure remains public.

All claims, failures, workflows and pins are public:
https://github.com/sushxnthd/theorica

## What the physical collaboration tests
Can a software-frozen scientific agent:
1. choose its own real measurements,
2. infer a quantitative relationship,
3. recognize instrument imperfections,
4. predict measurements it cannot query,
5. do so without a human steering the experiment?

## Rig 001
Planned automated optics bench:
- visible LED source;
- fixed polarizer;
- motorized analyzer;
- independent angle sensing;
- photodiode + transimpedance amplifier + ADC;
- microcontroller/tool API;
- append-only experiment logs.

The governing relationship and calibration are hidden from the agent.

## Scientific safeguards
The physical campaign is preregistered **before the rig exists**.

After START:
- no human hints;
- no human-selected measurements;
- no silent restarts;
- no deletion of valid failures;
- no tuning on the hidden physical holdout.

A failure to transfer will be released alongside a success.

## Sponsor recognition
A sponsor/lab may be acknowledged in the repository and technical report for equipment or facility support, but receives no control over analysis or conclusions.

## Minimum support that helps
Any one of the following is useful:
- loaned optics/electronics;
- access to a fabrication/maker lab;
- one researcher willing to review the apparatus;
- ₹25,000-equivalent in-kind hardware;
- a duplicate rig hosted at a second site for independent replication.
