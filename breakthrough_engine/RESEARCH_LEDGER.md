# Breakthrough Engine Research Ledger

## 2026-09-24: operator-family closure

Status: exploratory and isolated from main.

THEORICA already shows that cross-fitted residual spectral evidence can repair one missing continuous-frequency family. This branch tests the harder question: can residual structure prospectively identify which absent operator family is needed rather than applying a frequency-specific repair?

### H1

Given a deliberately misspecified base grammar and cross-fitted residuals, frozen family-specific diagnostics can select one missing operator family from a menu spanning periodic, exponential, rational/saturating, and logarithmic/power structure, improving extrapolation while maintaining a low false-expansion rate on laws already representable by the base grammar.

### Required controls

Before confirmatory holdout seeds are opened, freeze task generators, family menu, diagnostics, thresholds, complexity penalty, and seeds. Include a null panel representable by the base grammar. Compare unchanged base grammar, oracle family choice, a full enriched grammar available from the start, and random family addition under the same expansion budget. Primary outcomes are correct family selection, null false-expansion rate, extrapolation NRMSE, structural recovery where identifiable, and search/evaluation budget.

A positive result must beat random-family addition and materially close the gap to oracle/full-rich search without an unacceptable null expansion rate. Any diagnostic tuned after seeing confirmatory seeds invalidates those seeds.

### Novelty boundary

No novelty claim is made yet. Current literature already includes learned concept libraries, domain-aware operator priors, LLM-controlled operator/search choices, adaptive agentic symbolic regression, exhaustive/MDL search, and enriched vocabularies. The candidate contribution is narrower: cross-fitted residual diagnostics as a preregistered non-LLM mechanism for identifying a missing operator family under grammar misspecification, with explicit null false-expansion controls and prospective family-level evaluation.

### Holdout hygiene

Do not reuse the published periodic holdout seeds 1000-1099. Development and confirmation seeds for this study must be disjoint and recorded before confirmation.

### Next action

Inspect the existing grammar and spectral-closure implementation, then construct a development-only multi-family diagnostic panel on fresh seeds. Do not open confirmatory seeds until diagnostics and gates are frozen.
