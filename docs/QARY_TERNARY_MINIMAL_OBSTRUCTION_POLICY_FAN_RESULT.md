# Discovery-optimal finite policy fan: falsified before holdouts

## Result

The frozen finite-fan construction is false.  Exact policies synthesized on
the complete primitive discovery grid do not contain a policy attaining the
known exact value at the already-opened `(2,9,3)` counterexample.

For the first minimal obstruction pair `{(2,0,0),(2,1,0)}`:

```text
costs:                                  (2,9,3)
exact value:                                  51
best discovery-policy envelope:              52
best stored policy source costs:         (1,1,1)
```

This is a stricter negative result than failure of the single face `(2,5,1)`.
The retained mechanism used every distinct Pareto terminal-exposure antichain
from all 55 discovery optima, not a fitted coefficient.  It produced 34, 34,
and 33 distinct policies for the three obstruction pairs, respectively.  The
first family still needs a policy absent from all discovery-selected optima.

## Fail-closed evaluation

- exact discovery policies synthesized: **165/165**;
- planner states used for synthesis: **4,056,567**;
- prior cases replayed in frozen order: **181**;
- calibration matches: **180/181**;
- first and only mismatch: first pair at `(2,9,3)`, `52` versus exact `51`;
- remaining prospective holdout-family cases opened: **0**.

The duplicated prior `(1,4,2)` registration is replayed as three separate
ordered cases, so the calibration count matches the original 181-case record.
Because calibration failed, no prediction snapshot was emitted and none of the
20 still-unopened pair/cost cases was evaluated.

## Independent verification

A standard-library-only audit imports zero THEORICA modules.  It independently
recomputes every max-linear policy envelope, verifies every serialized exposure
set is a componentwise Pareto antichain, replays all 181 prior cases in order,
and checks the unique `52 > 51` failure and the absence of prospective rows.

The production synthesis itself also re-solves every discovery optimum before
extracting exposures; its source policies are executable planner trees rather
than inferred faces.

## Consequence

Policy switching remains the correct structural language, but sampling only
policies that are optimal on the small primitive grid is incomplete.  The next
experiment must change mechanism after two consecutive falsifications.  A
natural reformulation is a set-valued dynamic program that propagates
Pareto-minimal exposure antichains directly, rather than scalarizing first and
collecting only scalar-optimal policies afterward.  The unopened holdouts can
remain reserved for that genuinely different mechanism.

## Scope and limitations

This falsification is restricted to the three specified ten-word families,
depth-three ternary prefixes, four copies, one substitution, one recognized
erasure, and middle-first initial queries.  It does not show that no finite
policy fan exists.  It shows only that the fan generated by discovery-optimal
policies is insufficient, and it does not reveal the missing policy's complete
exposure antichain.
