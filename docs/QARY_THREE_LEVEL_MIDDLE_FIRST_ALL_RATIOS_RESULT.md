# All-positive-ratio middle-first adversary boundary

## Result

For every positive integer cost triple `(a,b,c)`, the binary, four-copy,
one-substitution, one-erasure problem conditioned on a middle-level first query
has the universal adversarial lower bound

\[
L(a,b,c)=\max\{a+5b+c,\;2a+3b+4c,\;2a+4b+3c,
3a+3b+3c,\;3a+4b+c,\;4a+2b+c\}.
\]

Equivalently, writing `(a,b,c)=(pu,qu,y)`, the complete projected adversary
antichain at every positive rational ratio `p/q` is generated by the six frozen
exposure vectors

```text
(1,5,1), (2,3,4), (2,4,3), (3,3,3), (3,4,1), (4,2,1).
```

This is a theorem-level impossibility boundary. It is an exact optimum on all
previously proved ratio-at-least-two slices and on the 30 frozen moderate-ratio
holdouts, but this result does not claim universal matching upper policies in
the moderate-ratio regime.

## Exact evidence

- The exact adversary recursion visited 178,964 states at each of 19 trace
  points: all nine positive structural boundaries and all ten open cells.
- All 19 projected antichains matched the six-face prediction.
- All ten open cells contained zero unaccounted affine comparison crossings.
- The union of realized sources recovered all six exposure vectors.
- All 30 frozen prospective planning instances matched `L` exactly across ten
  noninteger ratios and cheap, interior, and expensive leaf regimes.
- A standard-library audit imported zero THEORICA modules and reproduced all
  19 trace certificates and all 30 exact planning costs.
- Raw and audit artifacts reproduced byte-for-byte; the raw trace corpus is
  stored as deterministic gzip with a separate human-readable summary.
- Full local regression passed 274/274 tests; scoped Ruff and diff checks pass.

## Preserved negative result

The first verifier compared raw exposure representatives at structural
boundaries and failed 3/19 records. At ratios `1/2`, `1`, and `2`, two frozen
faces have identical projected coordinates, so the recursion legitimately
retains only one representative. The corrected check compares projected
antichains and records all three representative collapses explicitly. No
frozen kill criterion was relaxed: there was no extra face, interior crossing,
exact-cost mismatch, or audit mismatch.

## Executable consequence

`certify_binary_middle_first_all_ratio_lower_bound(p,q,u,y)` returns the six
face values, their maximum certified lower bound, and the active faces for any
positive rational ancestor ratio encoded with positive integers. Invalid
ratios or costs fail closed.

## Limitations

The result is confined to the conditioned binary policy class, four copies,
and one substitution plus one erasure. Universal moderate-ratio upper policies,
unrestricted first actions, leaf-first conditioning, higher arity, deeper
trees, other fault budgets, external proof review, and novelty review remain
open.
