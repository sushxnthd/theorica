# Four-vertex additive arrangement protocol

Frozen before implementation or outcome inspection.

## Question

For a finite robust game whose joint action is a Cartesian product of component
actions and whose payoff is additive across components, can a four-scenario
optimal policy be computed exactly without enumerating all joint actions?

## Proposed law

Let
[
D(q)=sum_tmax_{a_t}langle q,u_{t,a_t}angle,qquad qinDelta_4,
]
and let (L) be the number of distinct component-local action-tie hyperplanes.
The minimum of (D) is attained at a vertex of the arrangement generated by
those hyperplanes and the four facets of (Delta_4). Consequently an exact
solver needs at most
[
inom{L+4}{3}
]
candidate intersections, independent of (prod_t |A_t|). A primal optimal
policy with support at most four can be recovered from joint responses tied at
the minimizing nature distribution.

## Strong alternative explanation

The three-scenario result may be a low-dimensional accident. In a tetrahedron,
a convex piecewise-affine minimum could require information from expanded joint
actions, or component-local ties might omit a necessary global intersection.

## Frozen evaluation

1. Exhaust all two-component/two-action games with payoff vectors in
   ({0,1}^4): (16^4=65,536) games.
2. Compare the product-free solver against an independently implemented exact
   expanded-joint-action arrangement solver.
3. Check exact value, deterministic-versus-randomized classification,
   certificate validity, and support at most four.
4. Freeze 40 unseen mixed-shape integer-payoff games after implementation:
   20 pure-optimal and 20 strict-randomization cases.
5. Run an independent Fraction-only checker that imports no production code.
6. Include a scaling witness whose Cartesian action count is operationally
   infeasible but whose local arrangement remains small.

## Publishable threshold

- zero discrepancies in all 65,536 opened games;
- zero discrepancies in 40/40 frozen holdouts;
- exact independent audit passes;
- returned support never exceeds four;
- explicit counterexamples to nonadditive payoffs and uncertified inputs;
- full regression and hosted reproduction pass.

## Kill condition

Kill or reformulate this kernel on any exact value mismatch, any support-bound
violation, or if the number of candidates depends on the Cartesian joint-action
count. Preserve every failure.

## Smallest intended claim

A fixed-four-scenario, product-free exact planner and its candidate bound for
certified additive finite games. This is not a claim that hyperplane
arrangements, finite minimax, or sparse-support matrix-game results are new.
